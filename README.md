## 🌟 What is KHOJ AI?
KHOJ AI solves the superficial tourism problem. Instead of generic itineraries and top-10 lists, KHOJ AI creates personalized real-world discovery quests with physical on-site observation challenges (Local Detective) and verified local backstories.

### The 3-Step Discovery Loop
1. **FIND:** Personalized places based on your time (30m to full day), budget (₹0 to ₹2500+), and interests.
2. **NOTICE (Local Detective):** On-site challenge to look closer at what most visitors walk past.
3. **UNDERSTAND:** Reveal the verified historical context and oral tradition.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, React Router
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, Serverless Lambda Functions
- **Knowledge Base:** MongoDB & Mongoose (20+ verified destinations in Ambala, Chandigarh, Kurukshetra, Pinjore)
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini (personalized quests & storytelling with verified boundaries)
